# CNC Feeds & Speeds Calculator: FREE vs PAID Feature Comparison

**Comprehensive Analysis of Monetization Strategies & Feature Paywalls**

Generated: November 11, 2025

---

## Executive Summary

Four major CNC feeds & speeds calculator applications employ distinct monetization strategies:
- **FSWizard**: Freemium model (limited free tier)
- **G-Wizard Calculator**: Subscription with lifetime fallback limits
- **HSMAdvisor**: Hybrid free (trial + perpetual lite) with paid licenses
- **MachiningCloud**: Freemium with AI-powered tool selection (Premium)

The market shows a clear pattern: **material database size** is the primary paywall differentiator, with advanced calculations (deflection, power, tool life) reserved for paid versions in most products.

## MONETIZATION STRATEGY ANALYSIS

### Strategy Categories

#### 1. **Freemium with Material Database Paywall** (FSWizard)
- **Free Tier**: All features but only 3 materials
- **Paid Tier**: Same software, 200+ materials
- **Paywall Trigger**: Material limitation is immediately obvious
- **Conversion Lever**: Users hit limit when working with non-basic materials
- **Price Point**: $18.99-$29.95 one-time (low friction)
- **Value Proposition**: "Work with any material your shop uses"
- **Weakness**: One-time purchase means no recurring revenue

#### 2. **Subscription with Grandfathering Mechanic** (G-Wizard)
- **Free Tier**: 30-day full trial, then 2 HP fallback
- **Paid Tier**: Subscription-based ($79/year)
- **Paywall Trigger**: Spindle power limitation after trial
- **Conversion Lever**: Users with machines >2 HP are forced to upgrade
- **Unique Mechanic**: Subscription length directly increases permanent HP limit
- **Price Point**: $79/year (recurring revenue)
- **Value Proposition**: "Upgrade to maximize your machine's potential permanently"
- **Strength**: Creates incentive to subscribe longer (3-year options available)

#### 3. **Trial + Lite with Calculation Paywall** (HSMAdvisor)

## KEY MONETIZATION INSIGHTS

### 1. **What Gets Locked Behind Paywalls?**

**High-Impact (Material/Tool Access)**
- FSWizard: Material count (3 vs 200+) = most immediate paywall
- MachiningCloud: Tool database size (limited vs 560k+)
- HSMAdvisor: Calculation capability (1 material vs unlimited)

**Medium-Impact (Advanced Calculations)**
- G-Wizard: Spindle power limit (all features limited by HP)
- HSMAdvisor: Tool deflection, power, tool life calculations
- FSWizard: Chip thinning, HSM mode, full deflection
- MachiningCloud: AI tool selection, feeds/speeds optimization

**Low-Impact (Quality-of-Life)**
- G-Wizard: Export/reporting features
- HSMAdvisor: Tool request orders (free tier can still use tool database)

### 2. **What Never Gets Locked?**

- **G-Wizard**: Material database (all materials in free trial AND free tier)
- **HSMAdvisor**: Tool database and reference materials (completely free forever)
- **G-Wizard**: Geometry calculators, trig tools, reference materials (free forever)

**Insight**: Reference content and tool databases are positioned as "goodwill" to keep users engaged rather than conversion levers.

### 3. **Price Point Justification**

| App | Price | Target | Justification |
|-----|-------|--------|----------|
| **FSWizard** | $19-30 | Hobbyists, small shops | Low friction, try-before-buy |
| **G-Wizard** | $79-99/year | Small-to-medium shops | Annual recurring, feature-rich |
| **HSMAdvisor** | $199-349 | Professional shops | Advanced modeling, tool management |
| **MachiningCloud** | Custom (contact sales) | Production shops, CAM | Enterprise, volume discounts |

### 4. **Trial Strategy Differences**

| App | Duration | Features | Fallback |
|-----|----------|----------|----------|
| **FSWizard** | Perpetual | All features (3 materials) | Permanent free tier |
| **G-Wizard** | 30 days | All features | Hobbyist tier (2 HP) |
| **HSMAdvisor** | 30 days | All features | Lite tier (1 material, tools free) |
| **MachiningCloud** | Limited | Premium features | Lite tier (limited tools) |

**Strategy Pattern**: All apps offer perpetual or extended free access to hook users into ecosystem, then limit specific capabilities (materials, tools, power, calculations).

### 5. **Unique Conversion Mechanics**

**FSWizard**:
- Material hits immediately when users work with specialty materials
- Friction point is HIGH (only 3 materials in free version)
- Conversion path: Hit material limit → purchase Pro

**G-Wizard**:
- Spindle power limit is INVISIBLE until user needs more power
- Friction point is MEDIUM (2 HP limit requires larger machine)
- Conversion path: Exceed HP → purchase subscription → accumulate HP over time

**HSMAdvisor**:
- Single material is LOW friction for initial users
- But unlimited tool database keeps engagement HIGH
- Conversion path: Need multiple materials → purchase license

**MachiningCloud**:
- AI tool selection is VALUE-BASED paywall (not limitation-based)
- Friction point is LOW (users see value in AI, not blocked)
- Conversion path: Want optimization → upgrade to Premium

### 6. **Recurring vs One-Time Revenue**

| App | Model | Revenue Type | Sustainability |
|-----|-------|-------------|---------|
| **FSWizard** | One-time ($30) | Transactional | Lower (requires new customers) |
| **G-Wizard** | Subscription ($79/yr) | Recurring | Higher (customer LTV multiplies) |
| **HSMAdvisor** | One-time ($199-349) | Transactional | Medium (limited to professionals) |
| **MachiningCloud** | Subscription | Recurring | Highest (SaaS model + enterprise) |

**Insight**: Subscription models (G-Wizard, MachiningCloud) generate higher lifetime value. One-time purchases require larger customer base.

## Conclusion

The CNC calculator market exhibits clear market segmentation by price point and feature maturity:

1. **Entry-level ($0-30)**: FSWizard dominates with material database paywall
2. **Mid-market ($79-199/year)**: G-Wizard with subscription model and grandfathering
3. **Professional ($200-350)**: HSMAdvisor with advanced modeling and tool management
4. **Enterprise (custom)**: MachiningCloud with AI and CAM integration

The most distinctive monetization insight: **HSMAdvisor and G-Wizard deliberately keep core reference materials and tool databases free**, using them as engagement hooks rather than conversion levers. This builds user loyalty and ecosystem lock-in more effectively than pure restriction-based paywalls.

The emerging trend: **AI-powered optimization (MachiningCloud)** shifts paywall from "limiting capability" to "adding intelligence," representing the next generation of SaaS monetization in this space.
