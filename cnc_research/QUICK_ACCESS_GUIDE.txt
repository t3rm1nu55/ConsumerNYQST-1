CRITICAL FORMULAS (From Public Domain Sources)
================================================================================

1. CUTTING SPEED TO RPM (Metric)
   N = (1000 × V) / (π × D)
   Where:
   - N = Spindle speed (RPM)
   - V = Cutting speed (m/min) - from Machinery's Handbook tables
   - D = Workpiece diameter (mm)

2. CUTTING SPEED TO RPM (Imperial)
   RPM = (SFM × 3.82) / Diameter(inches)
   Where:
   - SFM = Surface Feet per Minute (from Machinery's Handbook)
   - Diameter = Tool or workpiece diameter

3. FEED RATE (Metric)
   F = N × f_z × Z
   Where:
   - F = Feed rate (mm/min)
   - N = Spindle speed (RPM)
   - f_z = Feed per tooth (mm)
   - Z = Number of flutes/teeth

4. TAYLOR TOOL LIFE EQUATION (The Foundation)
   V × T^n = C
   Where:
   - V = Cutting speed (m/min or SFM)
   - T = Tool life (minutes)
   - n = Material/tool constant (0.1-0.5)
   - C = Constant (from Taylor's research or material databases)

   Approximate values:
   - HSS: V × T^0.2 = C
   - Carbide: V × T^0.1 = C

5. SURFACE FEET PER MINUTE (SFM)
   SFM = (RPM × Diameter) / 3.82
