**What We Found:**

**The Most Damning Evidence:**

  - **This screams "tiny niche market"**

- **G-Wizard contradictory claims:**
  - Website claims both "15,000 users" AND "100,000 machinists"
  - The 100k is marketing inflation (includes all trial signups ever)
  - Real paying base: ~10-15k

- **No vendor discloses user numbers**
  - Successful SaaS companies shout their adoption metrics
  - The silence is telling

**Revised TAM:**
- **Global paying market: 20-35k users** (not 50k)
- **US market: ~8-12k users** (30-40% of global)
- **Penetration rate: 6-12%** of professional machinists use paid calculators

### 3. Comparable SaaS: PRICING IS TOO LOW

**The Devastating Reality:**

**Manufacturing Software Pricing:**
- Fusion 360: **$680/year**
- Mastercam: **$5,000+/year**
- SolidCAM: **$1,500-10,000**
- DNC/MDC software: **$400-1,000/machine/year**

**Vertical SaaS in Trades:**
- ServiceTitan: **$78,000 ARPU** (8k customers, $772M ARR)
- Housecall Pro: **$708-3,588/year**
- Jobber: Similar pricing tier

**Niche B2B SaaS Minimums:**
- Micro SaaS profitability: **$600-3,600 ARPU minimum**
- SMB vertical SaaS: **$600-6,000/year**

**Unit Economics at $199/year:**
```
ARPU: $199/year
CAC: $200 (content marketing, forums)
LTV: $600 (3 years × $199, assuming 33% churn)
LTV:CAC = 3:1 (barely viable, no margin for growth)

With 15% churn:
LTV = $199 / 0.15 = $1,327
LTV:CAC = 6.6:1 (better, but assumes low churn)
```

**Reality Check:**
- At $199 with $200 CAC, you **break even with ZERO margin**
- Manufacturing software CAC is typically **$2,000-10,000** (enterprise sales)
- You need $1,000-1,500/year ARPU to support real SaaS development
