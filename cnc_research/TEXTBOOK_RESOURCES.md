# Public Domain Textbooks & Academic Resources for CNC Feeds & Speeds Calculator

## Executive Summary

This document catalogues all publicly accessible textbooks, academic resources, and government publications relevant to building a CNC feeds & speeds calculator. Resources are categorized by public domain status, availability, and specific content relevance.

**Total Resources Identified: 30+**
**Top Tier Resources (Full Access, Public Domain): 10**

### 3. On the Art of Cutting Metals

- **Title:** On the Art of Cutting Metals
- **Author:** Frederick Winslow Taylor
- **Publication Date:** 1906 (presented to ASME as presidential address)
- **Public Domain Status:** YES - Published 1906 (pre-1928)
- **License:** Public Domain (US)

**Specific Content Relevant to Feeds & Speeds:**
- **Taylor Tool Life Equation:** V·T^n = C (cutting velocity × tool life to power n = constant)
- Empirical data from 26 years of experimentation
- 800,000+ pounds of steel and iron cutting tests
- 30,000-50,000 recorded experiments
- Cutting speed relationships for different materials
- Tool life prediction methodology
- Effects of cutting conditions on tool durability
- Feed rate effects on tool wear
- Material hardness relationships to cutting speed

**Where to Access:**
- **Google Books:** https://books.google.com/books/about/On_the_Art_of_Cutting_Metals.html?id=qhZDAAAAIAAJ
- **ResearchGate:** Various scanned versions available
- **Archive.org:** https://archive.org/search.php?query=taylor+metal+cutting
- **Print Reprints:** Available through various publishers

**Download Formats:** PDF, ePub, Kindle (through various sources)
